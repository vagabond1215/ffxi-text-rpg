# Zone Profile — Emberwash Badlands & Cinderwell Station

Status: **Data 55 canonical regional profile.**

## Role

Emberwash realizes the first canonical northern arid frontier south of Redstone without opening the farther true desert, southern strait, or Veyra sphere.

```text
Brasshaven / South Redstone Reach
  -> Cinderwell Caravan Road
  -> Emberwash North Wash
  -> Cinderwell Station [wagon limit]
  -> Saltpan Foretrail
  -> Emberwash Saltpan Verge
  -> farther salt-basin / true-desert approaches [not yet authored]
  -> southern strait port [not yet authored]
  -> sea crossing [not yet authored]
  -> Veyra northern port / heartland [not yet authored]
```

The zone is an arid frontier and preparation-sensitive travel barrier, not a disguised direct approach to the Veyra homeland.

## Places

### Emberwash North Wash

Danger 2 wilderness.

- dry Redstone upland breaking into ochre badland cuts;
- rocky grass, thorn scrub, gypsum shelves, and flash-flood channels;
- one maintained caravan road corridor;
- heat, dust, loose stone, and wash crossings matter;
- broad badland adjacency does not imply unrestricted southward travel.

### Cinderwell Station

Danger 0 travel/service locality.

Cinderwell is a small fortified caravan well and route station, not a city, southern gate capital, or Veyra border settlement.

Functions:
- field exchange;
- caravan warden / route desk;
- guarded cistern and water access;
- shade hearth and simple bunks;
- pack-animal watering and stabling;
- cordage/plaster/cistern repair support;
- last dependable ordinary-wagon turnaround.

### Emberwash Saltpan Verge

Danger 3 wilderness.

First salt-basin/desert-scrub edge of pale crust, saltbrush, dry channels, badland spires, dust wind, glare, sparse shade, and scarce dependable water.

There is no authored route onward toward the farther true desert, southern strait, or Veyra lands.

## Route contract

### Cinderwell Caravan Road

`route-emberwash-cinderwell-caravan-road`

- South Redstone Reach -> Emberwash North Wash -> Cinderwell Station;
- walk / mount / wagon / caravan;
- 13,500 yalms total;
- 5,400 seconds total;
- heat, dust, loose stone, flash-flood washes, scarce shade, and wash crossings.

Cinderwell Station is the ordinary wagon limit.

### Saltpan Foretrail

`route-emberwash-saltpan-foretrail`

- Cinderwell Station -> Emberwash Saltpan Verge;
- walk / mount / caravan;
- 8,000 yalms;
- 3,600 seconds;
- heat, salt glare, dust wind, soft crust, scarce water, and broken gullies.

No route exits Emberwash Saltpan Verge southward in Data 55.

## Food safety

Internal metadata remains explicit while world-facing language stays practical to the late-medieval/fantasy setting.

- raw Emberpod requires milling and cooking/baking before ordinary eating;
- ripe Cinder Pear is direct-ready only after peeling and removing fine spines;
- Ground Emberpod Meal still requires cooking/baking;
- Emberpod Trail Cakes and Dried Cinder Pear Strips are direct-ready.

No ordinary description assumes modern microbiology.
