# Zone Profile — Lower Deepvein & Lantern Sump Station

Status: **Data 56 canonical regional profile.**

## Role

Lower Deepvein realizes the first controlled Deep World frontier beneath the existing Deepvein Mine without opening a direct route to Korren gate country or the ancestral realm.

```text
Brasshaven Delvers’ Ward
  -> Deepvein Mine
  -> Lower Deepvein Haulage Decline
  -> Deepvein Lower Decline
  -> Lantern Sump Station
  -> Echoing Shelf Traverse
  -> Lower Deepvein Echoing Shelf
  -> farther deep roads / northern gate country [not yet routed]
  -> Korren trade/gate settlements [not yet authored]
  -> Korren ancestral citadel [not yet authored]
```

Lower Deepvein is a Brasshaven mining frontier and Deep World threshold, not a disguised Korren shortcut.

## Places

### Deepvein Lower Decline

Danger 3 dungeon.

A steep maintained-to-abandoned transition of hand-cut haulage galleries, timbered ledges, seep channels, salt bloom, glowmoss, loose stone, and natural cave openings beneath the existing mine.

### Lantern Sump Station

Danger 0 travel/service locality.

Lantern Sump is a compact staffed Brasshaven delver station beside a drained sump. It provides:
- field exchange;
- survey/route desk;
- lamp-and-repair workbay;
- cistern and common hearth;
- simple bunks and first-aid stores.

It is not a town, Korren gate city, or ancestral settlement.

### Lower Deepvein Echoing Shelf

Danger 4 dungeon.

A broad natural cavern shelf over black-water pools with mineral curtains, fungus banks, collapsed workings, and marked narrow ledges.

There is no authored route onward toward farther deep roads, northern gate country, or Korren lands.
